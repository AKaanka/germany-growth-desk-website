The problem

The German market needs more than remote outreach.

Many international manufacturers are ready for Germany, but not yet ready to open an office, hire a full-time local sales manager, or fly in for every meeting.

Customer access

Every visit depends on international travel

Potential buyers and existing customers need face-to-face attention, but sending your own team to Germany for every conversation is slow and expensive.

Trade fairs

Relevant events pass without local coverage

You cannot exhibit at or attend every German trade fair, so valuable conversations with buyers, distributors, and industry contacts never happen.

Local presence

There is no one on the ground

When a German company wants to meet, visit, or continue a commercial discussion, nobody local is available to represent your agreed business interests.

Execution

Research stops before the real work begins

A company list or market report has limited value unless someone contacts the targets, arranges meetings, walks through the door, and follows up.

The solution

Build a presence in Germany before building a German office.

Germany Growth Desk acts as your external local business development partner. We visit customers, attend meetings and trade fairs, conduct targeted outreach, and manage commercial follow-up — giving you practical support on the ground without immediately building your own German sales organization.

We do not just identify opportunities in Germany. We help you meet them.
  • Visit potential buyers, existing customers, distributors, and partners
  • Attend business meetings and relevant German trade fairs
  • Present your company and products within an agreed commercial scope
  • Document market feedback and move every next step forward
